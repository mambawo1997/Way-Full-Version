## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Way** is an innovative online cooperative game developed by **Coco & Co.** for **Windows** that challenges players to communicate and collaborate effectively. The **latest version** of Way brings improved gameplay and interaction, allowing you to engage in a unique experience that fosters teamwork. 

In **Way**, players embody two characters—an Eskimo and a Bedouin—who must work together to navigate through various levels. The **full functionality** of the game ensures players can immerse themselves in a rich educational experience while enjoying the thrill of cooperation.
